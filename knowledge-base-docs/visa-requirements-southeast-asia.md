# Visa Requirements for KelanaAI Destinations — Southeast Asia

_Last updated: August 2026. For Indonesian passport holders traveling on KelanaAI-arranged trips._

## Japan

- Indonesian passport holders require a visa for tourism stays.
- As of 2026, Indonesia participates in Japan's e-Visa pilot program for registered travel agencies. KelanaAI is an approved partner agency (partner code **JP-KLN-2091**), so trips booked through KelanaAI qualify for the expedited e-Visa track.
- Expedited processing time: **3 business days** (versus 5–7 business days for standard applications).
- Required documents: passport valid for 6+ months, KelanaAI itinerary confirmation, proof of funds (minimum IDR 15,000,000 in a bank statement dated within 30 days), return flight confirmation.
- Visa fee: **IDR 480,000** per applicant, paid directly to the Japanese consulate (not included in KelanaAI trip pricing).
- Multiple-entry visas are available for travelers who have visited Japan at least twice in the past 3 years, valid for 3 years.

## South Korea

- Indonesian passport holders traveling for tourism under 30 days do not need a visa if enrolled in the K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) system.
- KelanaAI pre-fills K-ETA applications for travelers as part of the booking flow; travelers must confirm submission within 48 hours of receiving the KelanaAI confirmation email.
- K-ETA fee: KRW 10,000 (approx. IDR 115,000), valid for 2 years from approval.
- Travelers staying longer than 30 days, or traveling for business meetings arranged outside the KelanaAI itinerary, must apply for a standard C-3 visa instead of K-ETA.

## Vietnam

- Indonesian passport holders can enter Vietnam visa-free for stays up to 30 days for tourism purposes — no e-Visa or K-ETA equivalent required.
- For KelanaAI group trips exceeding 30 days (available only on the "Extended Explorer" package), travelers must apply for a Vietnam e-Visa at least 10 business days before departure. E-Visa fee: USD 25.

## Thailand

- Indonesian passport holders can enter Thailand visa-free for stays up to 30 days.
- KelanaAI recommends travelers carry a printed hotel booking confirmation and a return ticket, as Thai immigration officers at Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang airports have been randomly requesting these documents since early 2026.

## Notes for KelanaAI Trip Planners

- Visa fees are **never** included in the `budget` field a traveler enters into KelanaAI — travelers should budget an additional 3–5% on top of their trip budget to cover visa costs.
- The KelanaAI support team (support@kelanaai.example) can provide a printable visa checklist per destination on request.
