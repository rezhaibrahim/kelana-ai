# KelanaAI Destination Guide — Yogyakarta, Indonesia

_Curated for KelanaAI travelers. Last updated August 2026._

## Overview

Yogyakarta (often shortened to "Jogja") is a Special Region in Java known for its royal heritage, batik culture, and proximity to Borobudur and Prambanan temples. KelanaAI classifies Yogyakarta as a **Standard** category destination for budget purposes, with typical daily costs ranging IDR 400,000–700,000 per traveler.

## KelanaAI-Recommended Itinerary Anchors

### Morning

- **Taman Sari Water Castle** — opens 09:00, entry fee IDR 15,000 for domestic visitors / IDR 25,000 for foreign visitors. KelanaAI travelers should arrive before 09:30 to avoid tour bus crowds.
- **Malioboro Street market** — best visited 07:00–09:00 for batik shopping before it gets crowded and prices rise.

### Afternoon

- **Prambanan Temple Compound** — entry fee IDR 50,000 (domestic) / USD 25 (foreign, cash or card). A combined Prambanan + Ratu Boko ticket is available for USD 30 and is the option KelanaAI itineraries default to.
- **Kotagede silver district** — recommended for travelers on the "Artisan Trail" add-on package; KelanaAI has a partnership discount of 10% at HS Silver workshop for travelers presenting a KelanaAI trip confirmation.

### Evening

- **Alun-Alun Kidul** (the south square) — known locally for the "masangin" tradition (walking blindfolded between two banyan trees). Free to visit, food stalls open until approximately 23:00.
- **Ramayana Ballet at Prambanan open-air theater** — performances run Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday evenings during the dry season (May–October), tickets from IDR 150,000. KelanaAI recommends booking at least 3 days ahead during July–August peak season.

## Transportation Notes

- KelanaAI's recommended transport for Yogyakarta trips is a chartered Avanza/Xenia with driver, typically IDR 450,000/day including fuel for in-city routes.
- The TransJogja bus network covers most tourist routes for IDR 3,600 per flat-fare trip, useful for budget/backpacker-category travelers.
- Borobudur is approximately 1 hour 15 minutes by car from central Yogyakarta; KelanaAI schedules Borobudur sunrise tours with a 04:00 hotel pickup.
