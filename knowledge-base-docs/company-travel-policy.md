# KelanaAI Company Travel Policy

_Internal policy document — version 3.2, effective August 2026. Applies to employees booking business travel through the KelanaAI platform._

## Trip Approval

- Trips with a total budget under **USD 1,500** may be self-approved by the employee and do not require manager sign-off.
- Trips with a total budget of **USD 1,500–5,000** require approval from the employee's direct manager before booking is finalized.
- Trips exceeding **USD 5,000** require both manager approval and Finance team review, submitted at least 10 business days before the planned travel date.
- Approval requests are submitted through the internal portal, not through the public-facing KelanaAI trip planner.

## Reimbursement

- Employees are reimbursed for actual expenses up to the approved trip budget; amounts exceeding the approved budget by more than **8%** require a written justification memo.
- Reimbursement claims must be submitted within **14 calendar days** of trip completion, with itemized receipts attached.
- Meals are reimbursed at a flat per-diem rate of **USD 45/day** for international travel and **IDR 350,000/day** for domestic travel, regardless of actual spend, unless a client dinner is pre-approved (in which case actual cost applies with a receipt).
- Alcohol is reimbursable only when part of a pre-approved client entertainment expense, capped at **USD 60** per event.

## Travel Category Guidelines

- Employees traveling for client-facing business meetings must book at minimum the **Standard** category in the KelanaAI planner; **Backpacker** category is not permitted for client-facing trips.
- **Luxury** category bookings require Finance pre-approval regardless of total trip cost, even if the trip is otherwise under the USD 1,500 self-approval threshold.

## Cancellations

- Trips cancelled more than 7 days before departure are eligible for a full refund of any KelanaAI service fees.
- Trips cancelled within 7 days of departure forfeit the KelanaAI service fee but any third-party (airline/hotel) refund eligibility is handled per that vendor's own policy.
- Employees who cancel a client-facing trip within 48 hours of departure without a documented emergency must notify their manager and the Finance team within 24 hours of the cancellation.

## Insurance

- All company-booked international trips automatically include KelanaAI's partner travel insurance (underwritten by Garda Trip Shield) at no additional cost to the employee, covering medical emergencies up to USD 50,000 and trip interruption up to the full approved trip budget.
- Domestic trips do not include automatic insurance; employees may opt in for IDR 25,000/day at checkout.
